- Palette retained from `app/src/main/java/com/ayashii/kuro/ui/theme/Theme.kt` and `Color.kt`: black background (#000), white primary text, subdued gray secondary text, dark neutral surfaces. The reference's red favorite heart is treated as a small functional accent, not a palette replacement.

- User-provided visual asset: `/root/desain-kurov2.png`; selected concept 2, “Modern & Elegant.”
- No Spotify or YouTube Music logos, brand assets, or copied exact visual assets are used.
